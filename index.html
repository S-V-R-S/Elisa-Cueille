About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Université Toulouse Capitole, conducting research within the SMAC (Systèmes Multi-Agents Coopératifs) team at IRIT.

My doctoral research is supervised by Benoît Gaudou, Nicolas Verstaevel, and Manon Prédhumeau.

Research

Thesis topic: Generation of agent behaviors under environmental constraints: application to prospective urban development scenarios.

Metropolises today face rapid urban growth and densification, raising critical challenges such as congestion, public transit load, air pollution, and environmental constraints. My work focuses on:

  • Multi-Agent Systems & Agent-Based Modeling (ABM): Simulating realistic mobility behaviors and activity generation.
  • Synthetic Populations & Activity-Based Models: Moving beyond purely historical survey data to model prospective planning scenarios and disruptive environmental events.
  • Multimodal Urban Mobility Simulation: Designing resilient transit algorithms and behavioral adaptation paradigms in urban contexts (e.g., GAMA Platform, MATSim).
